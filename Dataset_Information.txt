DATASET INFORMATION

Dataset name: NIJ Recidivism Forecasting Challenge — Training Dataset
Local project filename: data/NIJ_DataSet.csv
Original source portal (training dataset):
https://data.ojp.usdoj.gov/Corrections/NII-s-Recidivism-Challenge-Training-Dataset/8tjc-3ibv

Official NIJ challenge and codebook:
https://nij.ojp.gov/funding/recidivism-forecasting-challenge
https://nij.ojp.gov/funding/recidivism-forecasting-challenge-appendix-2-codebook.pdf

Modeling target: Supervision_Risk_Score_First (first parole supervision risk assessment score, 1–10). Scores are grouped 1–4 = Low, 5–7 = Medium, 8–10 = High.

Rows reported after removing records with a missing target: 17,698.

The original data contains identifying record IDs and sensitive criminal-justice-related attributes. Preserve the dataset's context and limitations. Before redistributing or using it beyond this educational project, check the source portal's terms, attribution requirements, and restrictions. The project does not establish that these data or predictions are appropriate for operational decisions.
